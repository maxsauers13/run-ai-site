Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. This policy covers the run-ai text-message service operated by Max Sauers LLC ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, so we can reply to you.
  • The text messages you send us and the replies we send you.
  • Running activity data you choose to upload or connect, such as workout files from your watch: distance, time, pace, heart rate, cadence, power, and location recorded during runs.
  • Rules and preferences you tell the service, such as which days you run.
  • Your email address if you sign in to the website.

How we use it

Only to provide the service to you: analysing your runs, answering your questions, and planning your week. Your messages and running data may be processed by an AI model provider to generate replies.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with anyone except the service providers needed to run the service (the messaging carrier and the AI model provider), and only to the extent needed to deliver it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while you use the service. Text STOP to stop receiving messages. To have your account and data deleted, email the address below and we will confirm when it is done.
