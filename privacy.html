Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. This policy covers Kip (formerly run-ai), a text-message running service operated by Max Sauers LLC ("we", "us"). Kip is invite-only.

What we collect

  • Your phone number and the date you opted in to texts, so we can reply and keep a record of your consent.
  • Your messages to Kip and Kip's replies.
  • Your name, email and home city, which you give by text. Your city sets your time zone and weather.
  • Running data from your watch account, once you connect it (COROS or Garmin): your activities and their workout files, with distance, time, pace, heart rate, cadence, power and the GPS route of each run, and the name of the device that recorded them.
  • Your watch account authorization: a token COROS or Garmin gives us when you approve the connection, and your Garmin user ID. We never see your COROS or Garmin password.
  • Calendar information, only if you connect a calendar: the times you are busy and the titles of those events, read-only. We never change your calendar.
  • What Kip learns or you tell it: your rules and preferences, your weekly targets and plans, and patterns computed from your runs (your usual paces and weekly shape, and an approximate home location, rounded to about 1 km, from where your runs start).

How we use it

Only to provide Kip to you: analysing your runs, answering your questions, planning your week around your calendar and the weather, and sending the texts you signed up for. We look at messages and logs only to fix problems and improve how Kip answers you.

AI transparency statement

  • What AI does here: Kip's replies are written by an AI model, Claude, from Anthropic. To write a reply, Kip sends Claude your message and the data needed to answer it: your recent runs and their details (including Garmin or COROS data), your rules, your plan, and the forecast. Kip's run analysis, weekly patterns and plan are computed by our own code; Claude puts them into words and answers your questions about them.
  • Why: so you can ask about your running in your own words and get a short, specific answer by text.
  • No AI training: we do not use your data, or anything derived from it, to train, evaluate or improve AI models, and we do not allow our providers to. Anthropic processes it only to produce Kip's replies.
  • Your consent: before Kip processes your running data with AI, it asks for your explicit OK by text, and does nothing with your running data until you reply AGREE.
  • Withdrawing consent, any time: text "delete my data" to withdraw consent completely and have everything deleted, or "disconnect garmin" (or "disconnect coros") to stop Kip receiving new data from that account. You can also remove Kip from your Garmin Connect or COROS account settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: email us, and we send you a copy of everything we hold about you (your runs and files, rules, plans and messages), within 30 days.
  • Correct: tell Kip by text ("that run was a workout", "my email is …") or email us.
  • Delete: text "delete my data". Kip explains what will be deleted and asks you to confirm. Once you confirm, everything we hold about you is deleted: runs, files, rules, plans, connections and messages, and Kip disconnects from Garmin and COROS. Copies in our nightly backups age out within 30 days. Twilio keeps its own record of texts under its policies.
  • Stop texts: text STOP at any time; START to resume. Text HELP for help.
  • Withdraw consent: see the AI transparency statement above.

We keep your data while you use Kip, and delete it when you ask.

Security

Data is stored on a private server with no open network ports, encrypted at rest. Watch and calendar logins are encrypted separately. Backups are encrypted in private storage. Only Max Sauers LLC has access.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes, and you will get a text about any significant change. Questions or requests: Max Sauers LLC, maxwellsauers@gmail.com.
