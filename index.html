run-ai

A personal running-analysis assistant from Max Sauers LLC. You text it after a run and it tells you what the workout was, how it compared to your own past runs, and how your week is going.

How to sign up

Text (872) 273-7477 from your phone. Sending a message is how you opt in. You can stop at any time by replying STOP.

What the messages look like

You: What was my last run?

run-ai: Your 3 x 3 mi is analyzed. Reps averaged 5:33/mi, 11 to 16 sec/mi faster than Aug 26 at about the same heart rate. Reply to ask more. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help.

You: How many miles do I have left this week?

run-ai: 45 mi left over 3 more days, target still 55.

Message details

Message frequency varies: typically one message after each run you record, one weekly planning message, and replies to anything you ask. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.
